- technician class info
  - have all amateur radio privileges above 30 MHz, including the very popular 2-meter band
  - can also operate Morse Code (CW) on portions of the 80m, 40m, 15m, and 10m bands, and voice and digital modes on portions of the 10m band
- other classes: general class (35 qs) and amateur extra class (50 qs)

- Electrical principles
  - current: the **flow** of electrons in a circuit, flows from positive to negative. measured in amperes
  - voltage: the **force** that causes electrons to flow in a circuit, measured in volts. also electromotive force, electric potential (represented by E)  
  - resistance 
  - alternating current (AC)
  - direct current (DC)
